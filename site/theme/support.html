Common questions

How do I download my theme?

After purchase you'll receive an email with a download link. You can also find it any time in your Account dashboard under Purchases.

How do I activate my license?

Open your theme's settings panel, navigate to License, and paste the key from your confirmation email. One key activates one site.

Can I request customizations?

Yes — chat with us and describe what you need. Smaller tweaks are often free; larger custom builds are quoted on a project basis.
