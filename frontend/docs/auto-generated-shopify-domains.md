---
title: Auto-Generated Shopify Domains
section: License & Domain
---

Every Shopify store gets a permanent domain in this format: `yourstore.myshopify.com`.

This domain remains attached to your store even if you later add a custom domain.

Your Vexel license is verified against this permanent Shopify domain to keep activation stable.

You can find it in Shopify Admin under **Settings → Domains**.
