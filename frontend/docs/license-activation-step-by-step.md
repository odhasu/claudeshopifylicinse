---
title: License Activation Step-by-Step
section: License & Domain
---

Activate your Vexel license from the Shopify theme editor in just a few steps.

1. Go to **Online Store → Themes**.
2. Click **Customize** on your active Vexel theme.
3. Open the **Vexel License** section.
4. Paste your license key exactly as received.
5. Click **Activate**.

If activation fails, confirm you copied the full key and that you’re activating on the correct store.
