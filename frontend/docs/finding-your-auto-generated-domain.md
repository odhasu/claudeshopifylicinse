---
title: Finding Your Auto-Generated Domain
section: License & Domain
---

Your auto-generated domain is the original `yourstore.myshopify.com` URL created with your Shopify account.

To find it, open **Shopify Admin → Settings → Domains**.

This domain cannot be changed, which makes it the most reliable identifier for license validation.

If you use a custom domain, your `myshopify.com` domain still exists in the background.
