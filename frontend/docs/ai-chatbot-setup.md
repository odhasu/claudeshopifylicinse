---
title: AI Chatbot Setup
section: Feature Guides
---

Enable the chatbot in **Theme Editor → Vexel Settings → AI Chatbot**.

Add a clear welcome message and a concise knowledge base with FAQs, shipping details, and policies.

Better input produces better responses, so keep your content specific and up to date.

After enabling, test it on your storefront to confirm the tone and answers match your brand.
