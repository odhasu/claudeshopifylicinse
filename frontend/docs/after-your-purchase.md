---
title: After Your Purchase
section: Getting Started
---

Right after your purchase, you should receive two emails: a payment receipt and a separate delivery email with your theme download link and license key.

Save your license key in a secure place. You may need it again if you reinstall your theme or move to a new store.

If the delivery email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam/promotions folder first.

Still missing it? Contact support with your order number and we’ll resend everything.
