---
title: Spin Wheel Setup
section: Feature Guides
---

Turn on the spin wheel in **Vexel Settings → Spin Wheel**.

Configure each wheel segment with labels and discount outcomes.

Then set your trigger behavior, such as time delay, exit intent, or scroll depth.

Use realistic rewards that improve opt-in rates without hurting margins.
