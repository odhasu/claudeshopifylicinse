---
title: Custom Domains and Your License
section: License & Domain
---

Connecting a custom domain (like `brand.com`) does not invalidate your Vexel license.

Your license is anchored to your permanent `myshopify.com` domain, not the custom one.

You can change custom domains whenever needed without reactivating your theme license.

If your store itself changes, contact support for a license transfer.
