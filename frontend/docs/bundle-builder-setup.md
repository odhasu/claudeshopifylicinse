---
title: Bundle Builder Setup
section: Feature Guides
---

Enable Bundle Builder from **Vexel Settings → Bundle Builder**.

Select which products can be bundled and define your discount tiers.

Common setup:

- Buy 2 items = 10% off
- Buy 3 items = 15% off
- Buy 4+ items = 20% off

Preview on product pages to verify pricing and layout before publishing.
