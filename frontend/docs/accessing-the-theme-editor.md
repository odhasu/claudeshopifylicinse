---
title: Accessing the Theme Editor
section: Theme Customization
---

Open the Shopify Theme Editor from **Online Store → Themes**.

Find your Vexel theme and click **Customize**.

The editor shows a live preview on the right and section/settings controls on the left.

You can click any preview element to jump to its related settings panel.
