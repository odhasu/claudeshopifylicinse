---
title: Understanding myshopify.com domains
section: License & Domain
---

Your `myshopify.com` domain is the permanent identifier for your Shopify store.

Even with a custom domain, Shopify still keeps and uses this original domain internally.

That’s why Vexel license validation is tied to `myshopify.com` instead of your custom URL.

You can always find it in **Settings → Domains** in Shopify Admin.
